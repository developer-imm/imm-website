Help and support

Can’t find your answer? Email support@interactivemm.com. We usually reply within one business day.

How do I cancel Traction Pro?

On iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Traction, and tap Cancel Subscription. On a Mac, open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings, and manage Subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

I got a new device. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with, open Traction Pro in the app, and tap Restore Purchase.

Do my tasks sync between my devices?

Yes. When you’re signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, Traction keeps your tasks in sync across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can’t issue them ourselves.

How do I delete my data?

Delete tasks in the app at any time. Deleted tasks are removed for good after 30 days. To remove Traction’s copy in iCloud, open your iCloud storage settings, choose Traction, and delete its data. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device.

We don’t keep your tasks on our servers, so there’s nothing for us to delete. If you’ve emailed us and want those messages deleted, just ask.

Is Traction a treatment for ADHD?

No. Traction is a productivity app designed with ADHD in mind. It isn’t a medical device and doesn’t diagnose or treat any condition.
