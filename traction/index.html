§ 01 / Features

Five quiet ideas, working together.

  1. № 01

    Ultra-fast capture

    Jot tasks instantly from anywhere in the app — no menus, no friction.

  2. № 02

    Smart recurrence

    Rule-based scheduling with projections. No endless duplicate items pile-up.

  3. № 03

    Sticky reminders

    Gentle, persistent nudges at intervals you choose — not a single push you'll miss.

  4. № 04

    ADHD-friendly UI

    Big tap targets, minimal choices, soothing motion. Built so the path of least resistance is the right one.

  5. № 05

    Apple-native, offline-first

    iPhone, iPad, Mac. Local data first, with iCloud sync — your tasks are yours, and they're always there.
