The short version

  • There’s no account to create. We never see your tasks.
  • Your tasks are stored on your devices and synced through your own iCloud account.
  • Speech recognition and Apple Intelligence suggestions run on your device.
  • Traction sends anonymous usage data, like which features get used, never your tasks. You can turn it off.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your information.

Who we are

Traction is made by Interactive MM LLC (“Interactive MM”, “we”, “us”), an independent software studio in Cincinnati, Ohio, USA. This policy explains what information the Traction app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac handles, and what happens to it. It also covers this website, interactivemm.com.

Your tasks and lists

Everything you put into Traction (tasks, lists, subtasks, notes, due dates, repeats, reminders, your streak, points, and stats) is stored on your device.

If iCloud is turned on for Traction, this data is also synced through Apple’s iCloud to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. It’s kept in your private iCloud database, which Interactive MM cannot access. Apple handles iCloud under its own privacy policy.

We don’t run servers that store your tasks, and we don’t require you to create an account with us.

Features that run on your device

Speaking your tasks

When you dictate a task, Traction uses Apple’s speech recognition on your device to turn your voice into text. The audio isn’t recorded, kept, or sent to us.

Apple Intelligence suggestions

Some features, such as subtask suggestions, first steps in What Now?, and understanding a task you’ve typed or spoken, use Apple’s on-device Apple Intelligence model on devices that support it. The text of the task is processed on your device and isn’t sent to us.

Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, and widgets

So you can find and act on tasks through Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, and widgets, Traction gives the titles of your open tasks and lists to the system on your device. This stays within Apple’s on-device features and is not sent to us.

Notifications

Reminders, including sticky reminders, are scheduled on your device as local notifications. They don’t pass through our servers.

Traction only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it, and works without any of them.

Anonymous usage analytics

To learn which features help and which need work, Traction sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service run by TelemetryDeck GmbH in Germany. A signal says something like “Quick Capture was used” or “a widget was added”, along with basic technical details such as the app version, operating system version, device type, and language and region settings.

Signals never include your tasks, lists, notes, or stats, and they carry no name, email address, or advertising identifier. TelemetryDeck replaces any device identifier with a hashed, anonymous value before it’s stored, so the data can’t be linked back to you.

You can turn this off at any time in Traction under Settings › Help & Tips › Share Anonymous Usage.

Traction doesn’t contain advertising, ad trackers, or third-party SDKs that track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Purchases

Traction Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes your payment, and we never receive your name, payment card, or billing details. The app receives a record from Apple confirming whether your subscription is active, so it can turn on Pro features. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by its privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and to help you, and we don’t add you to any mailing list.

This website

interactivemm.com uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as the pages you visit, your approximate location based on your IP address, and your browser and device type. Google processes this under its privacy policy. You can opt out with Google’s opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser. The Traction app does not use Google Analytics.

Sharing and selling

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising. The only outside services involved are the ones described above: Apple (iCloud, on-device features, and the App Store), TelemetryDeck (anonymous app analytics), and Google (website analytics). We may disclose information if the law requires it, but because we don’t hold your tasks, there is very little for us to disclose.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your tasks: delete them in the app whenever you like. Completed tasks are kept for 30 days and deleted tasks are removed after 30 days. To remove Traction’s data from iCloud, open your iCloud storage settings on your device, choose Traction, and delete its data. Deleting the app removes its data from that device.
  • Anonymous analytics: kept by TelemetryDeck in aggregate. Because it can’t be tied to you, we can’t single out or delete one person’s signals.
  • Emails to us: kept only as long as needed to help you. Ask us at support@interactivemm.com and we’ll delete your messages.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Union, the United Kingdom, and California, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict how it’s used, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Since your tasks stay with you and Apple, you already control most of your data directly. For anything we hold, such as emails you’ve sent us, contact support@interactivemm.com and we’ll respond within 30 days. We won’t treat you differently for using these rights.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in improving Traction for anonymous analytics (which you can turn off), and on your request when we answer your emails.

Children

Traction isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Traction handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, such as collecting a new kind of data, we’ll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
